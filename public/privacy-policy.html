Privacy Policy — Client Engine Chrome Extension

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Prominent disclosure: The Client Engine browser extension reads public page content on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp Web — including profile information, post content, usernames, follower counts, and captions — from inside your already-logged-in browser session. It then sends direct messages and posts comments on your behalf using the scripts you configure. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit your private message inbox or conversation history. You must explicitly consent on first run before the extension performs any action.

1. What the extension collects

  • Public page content on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp Web — profiles, posts, usernames, follower counts, captions — used to find targets and decide which actions to take.
  • Activity logs — actions performed (likes, follows, DMs sent, comments posted), timestamps, and target usernames.
  • Account information — your Client Engine email, display name, social usernames you connect, and subscription status.

2. How the data is used

  • Execute the automation actions you configure inside your Client Engine dashboard.
  • Track which DMs were sent so the dashboard can show delivery counts and avoid messaging the same person twice.
  • Display live session activity in the extension popup and on your dashboard.

3. Storage

Configuration, sent-DM history, and session state are stored locally via chrome.storage.local on your device. Activity stats are also synced to your Client Engine account so they are visible across devices on your dashboard.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising. Payment processing is handled by Stripe.

5. What we do NOT collect

  • Your Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp passwords. The extension operates inside your already-logged-in browser session.
  • Your private message inbox or conversation history. The extension does not read incoming DMs or chat threads.
  • Activity on websites other than Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp Web.

6. Consent & control

On first launch, the extension popup displays a disclosure overlay listing the data handled. The bot will not run until you click I Agree. You can revoke consent at any time by uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions.

7. Data deletion

To delete your account and all server-side data, contact admin@futnor.com. To clear local extension data, remove the extension from chrome://extensions or use Chrome's "Clear site data" option.

8. Contact

For privacy questions: admin@futnor.com
