Data & Privacy Disclosure

Before you use Client Engine, please review what this extension does with your data.

  • Reads public page content on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp Web (profiles, posts, usernames, follower counts, captions) so the bot can find targets and perform the actions you configure.
  • Sends DMs and posts comments from your logged-in account on your behalf using scripts you have configured.
  • Stores activity locally (sessions, sent-DM history, settings) in Chrome storage on this device.
  • Syncs activity stats (counts, timestamps, target usernames) to your Client Engine account to power your dashboard.
  • Does not collect your private message inbox or conversation history, and never sells your data.

By clicking I Agree you consent to the data handling described above and in our Privacy Policy.
